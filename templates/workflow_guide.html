Overview & Required System Inputs

This guide walks through all core compliance functions in this portal — specifying exactly what input files/data are required at every stage, where to navigate, and how bank statements, invoices, and GSTR-2B statements transform into the CA's official working papers and ready-to-upload GSTR-1 JSON returns.

Module 1: Income Statements, Output GST & GSTR-1 JSON Filing

Revenue & Output GST
1

Upload branch income statements Income & Output GST

Upload monthly branch statements. The system reads each account line, calculates net revenue as Credit Total − Debit Total, rounds to 2 decimal places, and automatically computes CGST (9%) and SGST (9%).

Required Inputs:
  • File Format: Branch Income / Trial Balance statements in Excel (.xlsx, .xls) or PDF (.pdf).
  • Batch Upload: Drag and drop individual branch files or an entire folder of branch statements simultaneously.
  • Period Selection: Target Financial Year (e.g. 2026-27) and Month (e.g. July).
2

Check the "Needs Review" notification banner Income & Output GST

After statement processing, an amber alert banner highlights items requiring auditor inspection — such as previously unseen GL/PL codes, manual-entry transactions, or locker/guarantee codes showing internal transfer accruals.

Required Inputs:
  • Click "Show these entries" on the yellow notification banner to filter and inspect only unclassified or flagged line items.
3

Classify GL/PL codes (one-time setup) Manage GL/PL Codes

Configure how specific account codes are treated across all branches. Unrecognized codes default to 18% Taxable for compliance safety — never silently exempting revenue.

Required Inputs:
  • GL/PL Code: Numeric or alphanumeric account code (e.g. 3230, 1878).
  • Tax Treatment: Taxable @ 18% (CGST 9% + SGST 9%), IGST @ 18%, or Exempt / Nil-Rated.
  • Entry Method: Auto-read (formula-driven) or Manual (allows typing custom figures).
  • Ledger Role: Normal Income, GST Payable Ledger (GL 1878/1879/1880), or Exempt Income Item.
4

Set manual figures & ledger closing balances Income Table & Ledger Section

For accounts with internal accrual distortions (e.g. PL 3230 locker rent), click the pencil icon in the table to enter the exact audited figure. Enter closing balances for bank GST Payable accounts (GL 1878/1879/1880) and exempt heads.

Required Inputs:
  • Manual Income Values: Click pencil icon on specific branch rows to override the formula.
  • GST Payable Closing Balances: Closing amounts for CGST (GL 1878), SGST (GL 1879), and IGST (GL 1880).
  • Cash Ledger Credit (Optional): Existing electronic cash ledger balance to offset net tax payable.
5

Export the CA Working Sheet (.xlsx) Income & Output GST

Click "Export CA Working Sheet (Excel)" to generate the comprehensive annual audit workbook with all branch tabs, Sheet1, Summary Sheet GST, and automated variance analysis.

Generated Output:
  • Branch Tabs & Sheet1: Complete breakdown of account income per branch.
  • GST Payable Reconciliation: Compares computed GST vs bank ledger with automatic red highlights for variances > ₹10.
  • Exempt Income Sheet: Month-over-month movement for all classified exempt revenue heads.
6

Upload B2B registered corporate invoices (optional) Income & Output GST

If the bank issued taxable services to companies or registered entities with a GSTIN, upload them here. The system maps them to Table 4 (B2B) and automatically subtracts them from retail Table 7 (B2CS) so revenue is never double-taxed.

Required Inputs:
  • File Format: Excel (.xlsx, .xls) or CSV (.csv).
  • Required Data Columns (Auto-Detected): Customer GSTIN (15 characters), Invoice Number, Invoice Date, Taxable Value, CGST, SGST, IGST.
  • Note: If no corporate invoices were issued for the month, skip this step — all income defaults cleanly to retail Table 7.
7

Generate & Download official GSTR-1 JSON Income & Output GST

Click the green "Download GSTR-1 JSON" button. A pre-filing preview modal summarizes all GST return tables. Verify the numbers and click "Confirm & Download JSON".

Required Inputs & Verification:
  • Bank GSTIN: Pre-filled 15-character GSTIN (e.g. 24AAACN0279M1ZL) — editable if filing for another branch registration.
  • Filing Period: Formatted filing code (e.g. 072026 for July 2026).
  • Generated Payload Tables:
    • Table 4 (B2B): Registered customer invoices with customer GSTIN, invoice details, and item rates.
    • Table 7 (B2CS): Net intra-state & inter-state retail banking service charges.
    • Table 8 (Nil/Exempt): Total exempt interest on advances and statutory non-taxable income.
    • Table 12 (HSN/SAC 9971): Mandatory SAC 9971 classification covering 100% of gross turnover.
8

Upload JSON directly to GST Portal GST Portal (gst.gov.in)

Upload the generated file to the GST Portal offline utility without any manual re-typing.

Portal Steps & Required Input:
  • Input: Downloaded GSTR1_<GSTIN>_<FP>.json file.
  • Step 1: Log in to gst.gov.in → Navigate to Returns Dashboard.
  • Step 2: Select Financial Year and Month → Click Search.
  • Step 3: Under GSTR-1, click PREPARE OFFLINE.
  • Step 4: Click the Upload tab → Choose File → select the downloaded JSON.
  • Step 5: Portal marks status as "Processed". Proceed to generate summary and file.

Module 2: GSTR-2B Input Tax Credit (ITC) Reconciliation

Purchases & ITC Matching
9

Upload purchase invoices & bills (Our Books) ITC Dashboard

Upload vendor purchase bills and expenses into the portal. The AI scanner extracts supplier GSTIN, invoice number, invoice date, taxable value, and tax head breakdown.

Required Inputs:
  • File Format: Vendor bill scans (PDF, JPEG, PNG) or Excel Purchase Book.
  • Captured Fields: Supplier Name, Supplier GSTIN, Invoice No, Date, Branch, State, Taxable Value, CGST, SGST, IGST.
10

Import monthly GSTR-2B portal statements Reconciliation Tab (Stage 1)

Upload the official monthly GSTR-2B Excel downloaded from the GST Portal. Gujarat and Maharashtra have independent upload panels so each state's returns remain separate.

Required Inputs:
  • GSTR-2B Excel File: Official GSTR-2B spreadsheet (.xlsx) downloaded directly from the GST Portal.
  • State Selection: Drop into Gujarat GSTR-2B Panel or Maharashtra GSTR-2B Panel.
  • Month & FY Selection: Select the filing month matching the GSTR-2B return period.
11

Inspect Reconciliation Ledger (New: Separate Tax Head Columns) Reconciliation Tab (Stage 2)

The reconciliation engine matches Books against GSTR-2B. Both desktop and mobile views show side-by-side breakdowns of Taxable Value, CGST, SGST, IGST, and Total GST.

Matching Categories & Visual Indicators:
  • Fully Matched: Books and Portal match by GSTIN, Invoice Number, and Tax within ±₹10 tolerance.
  • Value Mismatched: Invoice found in both, but amounts differ. The specific differing tax head (Taxable, CGST, SGST, or IGST) lights up in amber.
  • Possible Match: AI detects near-match (slight invoice number OCR typo or GSTIN character variation) with matching tax amounts. Confirm with one click.
  • Missing in Portal (GSTR-2B): Recorded in bank books, but vendor failed to file GSTR-1. Credit must be put on hold.
  • Missing in Books: Reflected in GSTR-2B portal, but missing from bank purchase books.
12

Export Filtered Reconciliation Report (.xlsx) Reconciliation Tab

Click "Export Filtered Ledger (.xlsx)" to download an Excel workbook formatted with separate tax columns and automatic categorization tabs.

Export Contents & Required Filters:
  • Required Filter Inputs: Financial Year, Month checkboxes, State filter (All / Gujarat / Maharashtra), Status filter.
  • 20-Column Layout: State, Supplier, GSTIN, Books Branch, Books Inv No, Date, Books Taxable, Books CGST, Books SGST, Books IGST, Books Total GST, Portal Inv No, Date, Portal Taxable, Portal CGST, Portal SGST, Portal IGST, Portal Total GST, Tax Difference, Match Status.
  • Categorized Multi-Tab Workbook: When exporting all records, the workbook automatically generates separate tabs for: Reconciliation Ledger, Fully Matched, Value Mismatched, Possible Match, Missing in Portal, and Missing in Books.
  • Accounting Totals: Automated =SUM(...) formulas at the bottom of every column on every sheet.
13

Follow up via Vendor Action Center Reconciliation Tab (Stage 3)

Take direct audit action on discrepancies to protect the bank from ineligible ITC claims and departmental interest.

Actions & Required Inputs:
  • Vendor Notice Draft: Click "Notify" on any discrepancy row to generate an automated discrepancy email or WhatsApp message listing missing invoice numbers, dates, and total tax credit put on hold.
  • Mark as Provisional: Put unreflected invoices on temporary provisional hold — the system automatically re-checks them against future months' GSTR-2B batches.

Frequently Asked Questions

What files are required to get the GSTR-1 JSON?

Only your normal branch income statements (PDF or Excel). If you have corporate registered clients, you can optionally upload the B2B invoice file. The app compiles Table 4, 7, 8, and 12 automatically.

How does the system ensure B2B invoices aren't double-counted in Table 7 (B2CS)?

The app automatically nets uploaded B2B supplies against total CBS branch income on the matching intra/inter-state sides. Table 7 only receives net retail banking transactions, preventing any duplicate taxation.

Can I preview the JSON summary before downloading?

Yes! Clicking "Download GSTR-1 JSON" opens a modal summary displaying the exact tax figures and turnover for Table 4, Table 7, Table 8, and Table 12 before downloading the .json file.

How do I get the separate columns for Taxable Value, CGST, SGST, and IGST in the Reconciliation report?

Simply navigate to the Reconciliation tab and click "Export Filtered Ledger (.xlsx)". The downloaded workbook automatically contains separate columns for Taxable Value, CGST, SGST, IGST, and Total GST for both Books and Portal, along with dedicated tabs for Fully Matched, Value Mismatched, and Possible Match.

What if the bank GSTIN changes or we need to file for another state?

You can edit the 15-character GSTIN directly in the GSTR-1 popup dialog before confirming download. The JSON envelope and filing period code update automatically.

A branch tab turned red in the CA Working Sheet export. What does that mean?

The GST the app computed from that branch's income differs from what the bank's own GL 1878/1879/1880 ledger says by more than ₹10. Check the GST Payable Reconciliation sheet for the exact numbers, then verify against the branch's original statement.
